Speak from Within

Our flagship 7-week public speaking program for ages 10 to 14, closing with a family Showcase Night where each child delivers a speech built from their own journey. This is not a performance class, children do not learn scripts or tricks. Over seven weeks they explore the emotional foundations of confident communication, self-awareness, self-regulation, empathy, and connection, grounded in Islamic values and evidence-based emotional intelligence research. Every session integrates prophetic guidance and Quranic principles alongside six core public speaking skills, building a voice rooted in identity, purpose, and character. Rooted

Our newest flagship, a 7-week program for ages 14 to 16 closing with Showcase Night, built entirely around Surah Ibrahim 14:24: a good tree has firm roots and branches that reach into the sky with no ceiling. The program takes that as its actual structure, not just a theme. Roots is identity and self-management, who you are before anyone hands you a title. Branches is vision, dreaming at full size and handing the how to Allah. Fruit is a real project, actually built. Harvest is standing up and saying it out loud to your own community. Every week surfaces a belief, breaks it open against a story from the Prophet, peace be upon him, and a companion, then tests the revised belief against a real Canadian teen scenario and a contemporary leader whose story echoes the same lesson. Teens leave with a vision document, a built thing, and a delivered speech, not a certificate of attendance.

Young Founders Program

A 6-week entrepreneurship and life skills program for ages 10 to 14, built on a simple belief: young people don't need to wait until adulthood to become founders. Each child begins with self-knowledge, understanding their natural founder type before any business content is introduced, then moves through what a business actually is, honest earning, real customer interviews, a full business model, brand, and selling as an act of service. Faith is woven through, not layered on top: founders learn that the Prophet, peace be upon him, was known as Al-Amin, the trustworthy one, because of how he traded. The program closes with Demo Day, pitching to a live audience of family and friends, and walking away with a portfolio, a founder certificate, and a business with a clear next step. Meets Thursdays.

Participants leave having spoken something true out loud in a room full of women who were listening.
